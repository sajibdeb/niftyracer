🎮 Getting Started

Welcome to NIFTY RACER – a Web3-powered endless racing game on Base where you can earn NiftyGold tokens!

  1. Connect Wallet: Click "Connect Wallet" to connect via Privy
  2. Get Your Car: Claim your starter car for 0.0005 ETH in the Shop
  3. Start Racing: Navigate to Play and hit "START RACE"
  4. Earn Rewards: Collect neons and achieve high scores
  5. Claim Tokens: Submit your scores on-chain and earn NiftyGold rewards

What is NiftyGold (nGOLD)?

NiftyGold (nGOLD) is the official in-game token of NIFTY RACER. Earn nGOLD by playing the game and submitting your scores on-chain!

Token Features

  • In-Game Currency: nGOLD is your reward for racing performance
  • Soulbound: Cannot be transferred between wallets (anti-bot protection)
  • Earned by Playing: Minted directly to your wallet when you submit scores
  • On-Chain Proof: Your nGOLD balance proves your gaming achievements
  • Base Network: ERC-20 token on Base blockchain

Current Use Cases

  • Achievement Tracking: Your lifetime nGOLD shows total gaming performance
  • Leaderboard Status: High earners gain recognition in the community
  • Player Reputation: nGOLD balance indicates dedicated players

💰 Reward System

How Rewards Work

  • Score Rewards: Earn 1 nGOLD per 1,000 points
  • Token Collection: Each collected neon in-game = 1 nGOLD reward
  • Car Bonuses: Better cars provide % bonus on earnings (up to 100%)
  • Max Per Game: 400 nGOLD per game (800 with Legendary car)
  • Daily Cap: 5,000 nGOLD per player per day

Reward Formula

baseReward = (score / 1000 + neonsCollected)
finalReward = baseReward × (100 + carBonus) / 100

🚗 Cars & Shop

Purchase cars with ETH to unlock bonus multipliers:

  • Starter - 0.0005 ETH - 0% bonus (Soulbound)
  • Speedster - 0.0033 ETH - 10% bonus
  • Turbo - 0.01 ETH - 25% bonus
  • Neon Beast - 0.022 ETH - 50% bonus
  • Legendary - 0.05 ETH - 100% bonus

Note: Starter car is soulbound (non-transferable). Premium cars can be traded on OpenSea.

🔒 Security & Anti-Cheat

NIFTY RACER uses multiple layers of protection:

Anti-Abuse Measures

  • Daily Cap: Maximum 5,000 nGOLD per player per day
  • Game Cooldown: 60 seconds between score submissions
  • Score Limit: Maximum 400,000 points per game
  • Neon Limit: Maximum 100 neons collected per game
  • Wallet Freeze: Cheaters can be frozen by admin

Soulbound Tokens

nGOLD tokens are soulbound (non-transferable):

  • Anti-Bot: Prevents farming bots from transferring to main accounts
  • Fair Play: Rewards stay with the player who earned them
  • Starter Car: Also soulbound to prevent claim-sell-reclaim exploits

Contract Security

  • ReentrancyGuard: Protection on all payable functions
  • Direct Treasury: All ETH payments go directly to treasury wallet
  • Mint on Demand: Rewards are minted when earned, no pool to drain
  • Verified Code: Contracts verified on Basescan
