🔒 PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: February 2026

1. Information We Collect

NIFTY RACER is a decentralized application on Base blockchain. We collect minimal data:

  • Wallet Address: Your public wallet address when you connect via Privy
  • Game Data: Scores, game sessions, and nGOLD rewards stored on-chain
  • NFT Ownership: Car NFTs you own (NiftyCar tokens)
  • Local Storage: Temporary game state and pending scores saved in your browser

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Display your game progress and statistics
  • Process reward claims and transactions
  • Maintain the leaderboard
  • Improve game experience

3. Blockchain Data

All on-chain data (scores, nGOLD balance, car NFT ownership, high scores) is publicly visible on the Base blockchain. This is inherent to blockchain technology and cannot be deleted.

  • nGOLD Token: Your balance and earning history
  • NiftyCar NFTs: Cars you own and their metadata
  • High Scores: Your personal best scores on the leaderboard
  • Transactions: All car purchases and score submissions

4. Third-Party Services

We integrate with:

  • Privy: Wallet connection and authentication
  • Base Network: Blockchain data storage (Layer 2 on Ethereum)
  • Farcaster: Social integration for miniapp features
  • OpenSea: NFT marketplace for trading premium cars

Please review their respective privacy policies.

5. Data Security

We do not store your private keys or sensitive wallet data. All transactions require your explicit approval through Privy's secure wallet interface.

  • No Server Storage: Game scores are submitted directly to blockchain
  • Soulbound Tokens: nGOLD cannot be transferred, protecting against theft
  • Smart Contract Security: Contracts include reentrancy protection

6. Local Game Storage

NIFTY RACER uses your browser's local storage to save game results until you're ready to submit them on-chain:

  • Offline Storage: Game scores are saved locally on your device after each race
  • No Server Upload: Your game data stays on your device until you choose to submit
  • Batch Submission: Play multiple games and submit all scores in one transaction
  • You're In Control: Choose when to claim your rewards on-chain
  • Easy Clearing: Clear pending games anytime via browser settings or by submitting

Storage Key: neon_racer_pending_games

7. Your Rights

  • Clear local storage data (including pending scores) through your browser settings
  • Disconnect your wallet at any time via Privy
  • Sell or transfer your premium car NFTs on OpenSea
  • View all your on-chain data on Basescan
  • On-chain data (scores, balances, NFTs) is immutable by design

8. Contact

For privacy concerns, reach out via our social channels linked on the main site.
